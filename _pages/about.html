I am a PhD student in computer science at Boston University, advised by Mayank Varia. My main research focus is building cryptographic protocols that increase privacy on the web. Currently I'm interested in:

  • Anonymous web services that can manage user data and identities without ever learning them (ePrint 2024/962)
  • Private information retrieval (ePrint 2026/1651)
  • TLS oracles, and more generally, running TLS in MPC (ePrint 2026/540)

I did my undergrad at Oregon State University in computer science and math. While there, I was fortunate to have worked with Mike Rosulek, Rakesh Bobba, and Huck Bennett.

I also serve on the cryptography advisory board for Callisto, a nonprofit building tools to help survivors of sexual assault.
